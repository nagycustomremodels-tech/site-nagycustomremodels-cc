Remodeling Services in Jacksonville, FL

Most remodels touch several trades — framing, tile, paint, trim. Hiring a different contractor for each one creates gaps, delays and finger-pointing. Nagy handles it all with one crew: one point of contact, one standard of work, from demo to final walkthrough.

01 / Kitchens

Kitchen remodeling

The kitchen is where your family gathers every day — and the room that sells the house. We plan the layout around how you actually cook, then build it out: custom and semi-custom cabinetry, quartz, granite or solid-surface countertops, backsplash tile, islands, lighting and appliance installation, with plumbing coordinated by the same crew.

North Florida humidity is hard on cabinet boxes and grout lines, so we use moisture-rated materials, seal every backsplash joint and set counters dead level before the faucet goes in. From San Marco bungalows to new builds in Nocatee, you get a kitchen that works harder than anything off a showroom floor.

  • Custom & semi-custom cabinets
  • Quartz, granite & solid-surface counters
  • Backsplash tile installation
  • Islands & full layout changes
  • Appliance installation
  • Lighting & plumbing coordination

02 / Bathrooms

Bathroom remodeling

A bathroom is only as good as what's behind the tile. Every shower we build gets a properly sloped pan and a continuous waterproofing membrane on the walls before a single tile is set — that's what keeps Florida humidity and daily steam out of your framing for the long haul.

On top of that we build the parts you see: walk-in and curbless showers, niches and benches, freestanding and soaking tubs, floating vanities, mirrors, lighting and fixtures. Guest baths in Mandarin, powder rooms in Riverside, primary suites in Ponte Vedra Beach — same crew, same standard.

  • Walk-in & curbless showers
  • Shower pans & wall waterproofing
  • Custom shower tile, niches & benches
  • Freestanding & soaking tubs
  • Vanities & countertops
  • Fixtures, mirrors & lighting

03 / Flooring & Tile

Flooring & tile installation

We install solid and engineered hardwood, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), laminate and porcelain, ceramic or natural stone tile. Every job starts with the subfloor: we check for moisture, flatten high and low spots and let materials acclimate, because coastal humidity is what makes a rushed floor buckle or gap two summers later.

Tile is only as good as the hands that set it. Our crew handles shower surrounds, kitchen and bath backsplashes, floor patterns and accent walls with straight lines, consistent joints and waterproof membranes where they matter — in homes from Jacksonville Beach to Orange Park.

  • Solid & engineered hardwood
  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)
  • Laminate flooring
  • Porcelain, ceramic & stone tile
  • Shower tile & backsplashes
  • Subfloor prep, leveling & transitions

04 / Decks & Exterior

Decks, balconies & exterior work

We build and repair hardwood and composite decks, second-story and rooftop decks, covered balconies and porches. Outdoors in Northeast Florida, sun, salt air and summer storms work on every board and fastener, so we pick materials and hardware for the exposure and stain and seal wood to keep it from graying and splitting.

The same crew handles the rest of the outside of your house: exterior painting with real surface prep, siding installation and repair, fences, and minor roof repairs before they become major ones. From Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach to Fleming Island, the exterior gets the same standard as the kitchen.

  • Hardwood & composite decks
  • Balconies, porches & rooftop decks
  • Deck repair, staining & sealing
  • Exterior painting
  • Siding installation & repair
  • Fences & minor roof repairs

05 / Built-ins & Finish

Wet bars, built-ins & interior finish

The custom details are what make a house feel finished: wet bars with wine fridges and under-cabinet lighting, butler's pantries with floating shelves, accent and slat walls. We install the cabinets, set the counters, run the backsplash and scribe every filler so it fits your walls — not a catalog drawing.

Finish carpentry ties it together. Baseboards, crown molding, door casings and decorative trim, plus interior and exterior door installation and replacement. Joints are tight, nail holes filled and caulk lines clean, so the paint looks crisp in a Riverside bungalow or a new home in St. Johns.

  • Wet bars & wine storage
  • Butler's pantries & floating shelves
  • Accent & slat walls
  • Cabinet & countertop installation
  • Baseboards, crown & trim
  • Door installation & replacement

06 / Whole-Home

Whole-home renovation, drywall & painting

When it's time to take a house down to the studs, you need a crew that can manage the full scope without losing momentum. We coordinate demolition, layout changes, electrical and plumbing rough-ins and every finish trade on one schedule, with one point of contact who knows where every room stands.

Walls and paint are where a renovation shows its quality. We hang, tape and finish drywall up to a Level 5 finish, patch and repair what's there, and prep every surface before paint — patching, sanding, priming — for crisp lines and even coats inside and out. Window installation and replacement included, from Fernandina Beach to St. Augustine.

  • Full-home planning & scheduling
  • Demolition & layout changes
  • Electrical & plumbing rough-in coordination
  • Drywall installation, repair & Level 5 finish
  • Interior & exterior painting
  • Window installation & replacement
