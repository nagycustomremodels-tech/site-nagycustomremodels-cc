Who we are

From passion to every last detail

Nagy Custom Remodels started with a simple belief: homeowners deserve a contractor who treats their house like his own. Founded in Jacksonville, the company was built one honest project at a time: showing up early, communicating clearly and never cutting corners. That reputation didn't come from marketing. It came from the work.

Craftsmanship isn't a buzzword here. It's why we measure twice, obsess over grout lines and stay late to get the trim right. Every kitchen, bathroom and floor carries our name, and we take that personally, from demolition to the final walkthrough.

Jacksonville is home. We know the neighborhoods, the building codes and the humidity and salt air that punish cheap materials. And because this is a diverse city, our team speaks English and Portuguese, so every homeowner gets clear communication from estimate to completion.
