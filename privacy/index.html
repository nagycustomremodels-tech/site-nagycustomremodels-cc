Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Who we are

Nagy Custom Remodels is the trade name of Nagy General Services LLC ("we", "us", "our"), a licensed and insured remodeling contractor based in Jacksonville, Florida. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit nagycustomremodels.com or contact us through it.

2. Information you send us

Our booking form does not store anything on a server. When you submit it, your device opens a text message (iMessage or SMS) addressed to us, pre-filled with what you typed:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The type of project, your city or neighborhood
  • Your preferred consultation date and time, and any project details

Nothing is sent until you tap Send in your messaging app. If you call or text us directly, we receive your phone number and whatever you share in the conversation.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your request and schedule an on-site consultation
  • To prepare your estimate and follow up about your project

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Cookies, analytics & browser storage

We use Google Tag Manager to manage measurement and advertising tags (for example Google Analytics, Google Ads or Meta Pixel). These tools may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure visits and ad performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads through Google Ads Settings.

5. Third-party services

This site links to or loads services such as Google (Tag Manager, Google Business Profile) and Instagram. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

6. Data retention

Messages and call records stay in our normal business communication records for as long as needed to serve you or as required by law.

7. Your choices

Contact us at any time to ask what information we have about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop follow-up messages.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
